# 📈 Viral Growth Playbook — Scary Shorts for Tier-1 (USA) Audiences

Research-backed strategy (2026). Read this before posting — the tool makes the
video, but THIS makes it go viral.

---

## 1. How the YouTube Shorts algorithm actually works (2026)

The algorithm runs in **two phases**:

1. **Explore** — your Short is shown to a small *seed* audience.
2. **Exploit** — if that seed group does NOT swipe away, distribution scales up
   fast (you can blow up with **zero subscribers**).

**The numbers that decide your fate:**
- **Swipe-away rate** — if **>40%** swipe away in the first hour, distribution
  **stops**. If ~70% watch through, you get pushed aggressively.
- **Completion rate beats watch time.** A 20s Short at 90% completion beats a 60s
  Short at 70%. YouTube optimizes for *"satisfaction per swipe."*
- **Loop rate, shares, and first-few-seconds engagement** are now top signals.
- **Viral window is only 24–36 hours** — early signals make or break it.

👉 **What this means for you:** the **first 1–3 seconds** and the **ending loop**
matter more than anything else.

---

## 2. ⚠️ The length truth (important for your 40s+ goal)

Research says the algorithm **favors 20–30s** Shorts; retention drops sharply
**after ~45s**. You want 40s+, which is OK **for stories** (people stay for a
narrative), but you MUST fight the drop-off:

- Keep it **40–45s, not 60s.** (Tool default = 45s. Don't go higher.)
- **Hook at 2.5–3s** AND a **second hook at ~14–15s** (a new twist/threat).
- **Cut visuals every 2–3 seconds** — never let a shot get boring.
- **End on a loop** — last line should make people rewatch from the top
  (loops = massive algorithm boost).

---

## 3. The viral HOOK formula (first 3 seconds)

85% of Shorts are watched **muted** at first → your hook must work with
**captions + visuals**, not just voice.

Great scary hooks (first spoken line, also shown as big text):
- "Never answer your door after 3 AM."
- "The baby monitor caught something that wasn't my son."
- "My Uber driver locked the doors."
- "I work night shift alone. Last night I wasn't alone."

Rules: **max 12 words, present tense, instant dread or a question.** (Your
`prompts/scary_story.txt` already forces this.)

## 5. Posting & growth tactics

- **Post 1–3 Shorts per day.** Daily posting = **3–5× more** distribution than
  weekly. Consistency is a ranking factor.
- **Best time for USA:** evenings **6–10 PM EST** (also catches west coast).
- **First hour is everything** — share to a few places to spark early engagement.
- **Series format** ("Part 1", "Story #14") trains viewers to follow & binge.
- **Reply to every early comment** in the first hour (boosts engagement signal).

---

## 6. Titles, captions, thumbnail (the tool helps here)

- **Title:** curiosity + keyword. e.g. *"He shouldn't have opened the door… 😨
  #scary #horrorstories"*
- **Captions:** ALWAYS on, big, word-by-word highlight (the tool does this) —
  critical because most people watch muted.
- **Thumbnail:** the tool auto-makes a bold yellow-text cover. Use a **shocked
  face / dark scene + 2–4 huge words.**
- **Hashtags:** `#scary #horror #scarystories #creepy #shorts` (3–5 max).

---

## 7. Audio quality (for "best quality" voice + music)

- Voice: `am_michael` or `am_adam` (deep American male), **speed 0.90–0.95** for
  drama. The tool normalizes audio to **-14 LUFS** (YouTube's loudness standard)
  so it sounds clear and "pro," never quiet.
- Music: **dark ambient/drone at volume 0.10–0.14** (set in `config.yaml`) so it
  sets mood without burying the voice.
- Only use **royalty-free** music (YouTube Audio Library, Pixabay) — copyrighted
  tracks = no monetization + strikes.
